ThermophysicalDB.jlExperimental thermophysical data, one call away

Download thermophysical databases once, keep them up to date, and filter, plot and analyse more than a hundred thousand measured systems from the Julia REPL. Initially only ThermoML is included, but the idea is to add more databases in the future.

Get started
Tutorials
View on GitHub

One call gets everything

db = database() downloads all databases validated for the package, verifies its SHA-256 (or equivalent), parses it once and keeps a binary cache. Later sessions only check for a newer archive and load the cache.

Filter the way you think

Select by compound, property, temperature and pressure range, author, DOI, year and more. Chain filters; each one only touches the rows that survived the previous one.

Units that mean something

Limits accept Unitful quantities such as 25u"°C" or 1u"atm", and tables can hand you Unitful columns, in the units of the source or in SI.

Uncertainty on one footing

Every expanded uncertainty is also restated at k = 2, with an explicit and documented rule, so points from different laboratories can be weighted consistently.

Tables.jl all the way

A selection is a Tables.jl table made of plain vectors. Plot the columns with Makie, hand them to a DataFrame, or write them to disk, without conversion code.

Two databases, one search

The experimental points (ThermoML) and the nomenclature of the compounds (names, CAS, PubChem CID, SMILES, InChI, molar mass) are two separate databases, linked on demand. Name a compound by any of them; when a name is ambiguous you choose.

Extensible and versatile

Capacity to add your own data to a local database formatted according to the same standard used in other databases. Customize the parameters of interest and how to interact with them.

Data source ​

The data, for now, comes only from the ThermoML Archive, created and maintained by the Thermodynamics Research Center (TRC) of the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) and based on the IUPAC ThermoML standard. ThermophysicalDB.jl is an independent project that downloads, reads and filters that archive; it does not own or redistribute the data, and it never changes the published values: conversions and restated uncertainties are produced on request, apart from them. The goal of the package is to bring several experimental databases under one interface, and the ThermoML Archive is the first one supported.

  • Dataset: doi:10.18434/mds2-2422

  • Standard: (Frenkel et al., 2006); background in the series of (Frenkel et al., 2003), (Chirico et al., 2003), (Frenkel et al., 2004), (Chirico et al., 2009) and (Frenkel et al., 2011)

  • More details, the terms of use and how to cite: About the data

The package keeps two databases, side by side and never mixed. The thermophysical database holds the experimental points and is the reference: it is never changed. The compound database holds the nomenclature and the base properties of the compounds (all the names, the CAS number, the PubChem CID, the formula, the molar mass, the SMILES, the InChI and the InChIKey) and is built from lists derived from PubChem; the compounds you add are kept in a separate file of your own and loaded together with the lists. link_compounds joins them, so that a compound of the archive can be found by any of those names. See Compounds and their identifiers.
